Who we sponsor

We work with projects that are:

  • Biocentric in their values and approach
  • Grassroots and community-led
  • Working on ecological protection, habitat defense, Indigenous land rights, or related social justice issues
  • Acting in good faith with the communities their work affects
  • Not duplicating existing, well-funded efforts

We've sponsored projects across the western United States and internationally, including mining resistance, urban permaculture, climate grief, Zen ecological practice, and rural agroforestry. Groups defending a specific place through public comment, watchdog work, or legal intervention are welcome.

How it works

  1. Step 1 · Apply

    Tell us about the project

    Fill out the application below: what you do, who's involved, and how you plan to fund it.

  2. Step 2 · Talk

    Check that it's a fit

    We review applications on a rolling basis, usually reply within three weeks, and set up a call.

  3. Step 3 · Start

    Sign and receive funds

    We sign a sponsorship agreement, and donations and grants start flowing through FGC.

What's included

  • 501(c)(3) statusTax-deductible donations and eligibility for foundation grants
  • Financial administrationWe receive, hold, and pay out funds with full accounting
  • Tax complianceInclusion in our 990 and donor acknowledgment letters
  • Quarterly financial reportsClear statements of income and spending
  • Grant writing supportHelp drafting and reviewing applications
  • Donor communicationsAppeals, acknowledgments, and updates
  • Policy templatesConflict of interest, whistleblower, document retention
  • Annual report helpA narrative and financial summary for your funders

We keep 9% of the funds a project receives to cover administration. The rest goes to your work.
