About the event

From 2010 to 2014, we organized the Earth at Risk conference series. It brought together leaders from Indigenous, feminist, anti-war, anti-racist, and environmental movements to talk about protecting the living world and challenging the economic system that threatens it.

More than 1,000 people attended over the years. Speakers included Arundhati Roy, Vandana Shiva, Alice Walker, Chris Hedges, Derrick Jensen, Lierre Keith, and Dominique Christina.
