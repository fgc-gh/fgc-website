Our mission, as reported

To conserve ecologically and culturally significant land in Washington, Oregon, and California.

That year our return described us as "a biocentric land trust rooted in fifteen years of grassroots organizing" that protects land and provides fiscal sponsorship to grassroots ecological and social justice initiatives.

This is how we described our work to the IRS that year. We have since stepped back from holding land to focus on fiscal sponsorship and campaign help.

About these figures

These figures come from our 2025 IRS return (Form 990), prepared by our accountant from our records. They are not audited financial statements.

The public copy of the return leaves out the schedule of contributors, which names individual donors. The law allows us to keep donor names private.
