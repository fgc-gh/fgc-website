Land

Holding land for the life that depends on it

In short

  • We own about 15 acres along Elk Creek in Del Norte County, California, and keep it protected.

Elk Creek

Where
Del Norte County, California
Size
About 15 acres
Habitat
Forest along the creek

Our land sits where forest meets water. Shady banks keep the creek cold enough for coho salmon. Roots and fallen logs hold the soil and give young fish places to hide.

Who lives here

  • Coho salmon (threatened)
  • Northern spotted owl (threatened)
  • Port Orford cedar
  • Pacific giant salamander
  • Roosevelt elk
  • Cougar and black bear

Elk Creek is at the coastal edge of the Klamath-Siskiyou region, one of the richest temperate forests on Earth. The land doesn't need much managing. It needs to be kept out of harm's way.

Ways to protect your land

There are three main options. We can help you figure out which one fits.

Comparison of three ways to protect land
OptionWhat it isWho owns the landWhen it starts
Conservation easementA legal agreement that permanently limits building and other harm on the land. We check on it over time.You doNow
Land donationYou give the land to us, and we hold it permanently for the life that depends on it.We doNow
BequestYou leave the land to us in your will.You do, for your lifetimeAfter your death

Each option may have tax benefits. Talk with a tax advisor about your situation.
