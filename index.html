Cascadia — Est. 2009 Defend · Sponsor · Hold

Wild places are being lost in administrative proceedings most people never hear about — in permit decisions made without challenge, in scoping windows that close before anyone responds. Fertile Ground Conservancy works in those processes, holds land permanently, and sponsors other grassroots initiatives doing similar work. We are a biocentric 501(c)(3).

You have skills to contribute

Federal land management agencies process thousands of decisions a year. Most pass without substantive ecological challenge — not because it isn't warranted, but because the capacity to mount it doesn't exist. We are building that capacity. If you have legal, scientific, or writing skills and want to put them to use in the administrative record, we want to hear from you.

501(c)(3) nonprofit · EIN: 27-1194001

We operate on the unceded territories of the Tolowa, Yurok, Karuk, and other Indigenous peoples of the Pacific Coast. These nations have lived in constitutive relationship with this land for thousands of years — not managing it, not owning it, but belonging to it in a way that shaped their language, governance, ceremony, and body of knowledge. What FGC is trying to practice, they have been practicing since long before conservation was a word. We are working to act in a manner consistent with that prior claim, and we fall short of it regularly. We name this not as ceremony but as accountability.
